# Park et al. (2024) — *Generative Agent Simulations of 1,000 People*

**Citation**: Park, J. S., Zou, C. Q., Shaw, A., Hill, B. M., Cai, C., Morris, M. R., Willer, R., Liang, P., & Bernstein, M. S. (2024). "Generative Agent Simulations of 1,000 People."

**Status**: stub from prior knowledge. Verify before citing externally.

## Core argument

Stanford follow-up to the original *Generative Agents* paper (Park et al., 2023), this work scales generative-agent simulation to ~1,000 personas conditioned on extensive interview-derived backstories of real individuals. The agents reproduce a substantial portion of the GSS-style attitudinal variance of their human counterparts. Adding behavioral and interview-derived backstory data improves fidelity over demographics-alone conditioning.

## Why it matters for FORUM

Closest neighbor in the literature in terms of *scale* and *intent*. Provides:
- Empirical evidence that LLM-driven persona simulation scales beyond toy experiments
- A methodology for richer persona conditioning (interview-derived backstories) that may be useful in v2 of FORUM if/when interview corpora are available
- A validity benchmark: how much of human attitudinal variance can be reproduced under different conditioning regimes

## What FORUM borrows

- Confidence that 100-persona deliberations are tractable and meaningful
- The empirical observation that richer persona conditioning improves fidelity (motivates v2 priors expansion)

## What FORUM does not adopt

- Park et al. condition on interview transcripts of real individuals. FORUM cannot do this for ethical reasons (no real-individual conditioning). The k-anonymity-enforced ACS+ANES sampling is the deliberate alternative.
- Park et al. are doing *opinion simulation*, not *deliberative-outcome simulation*. The deliberation step changes what is being measured.

## Open questions for the FORUM project

- Can the demographic-cell-conditioned approach reproduce a meaningful fraction of the variance Park et al. recover via interview-conditioning, or is interview-conditioning fundamentally more informative?
- What's the calibration cost of the privacy posture (k≥5 sampling, no real-individual conditioning)?
