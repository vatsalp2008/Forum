# Lit review

Each memo here is a working note on a foundational source. The memos are scoped to ~1 page and are intended for the FORUM author's own reference; they are not authoritative summaries.

**Honest scope note**: these v0 memos are stubs written from prior knowledge of the literature. Before any external publication or pilot pitch that cites them, every memo should be verified by re-reading the source and expanded with direct page references. They are committed in this state to bound Phase 0 scope; they are not yet defensible scholarship.

The 6 must-reads, in recommended order:

1. [Argyle et al. (2023) — Out of One, Many](argyle-2023.md) — most direct precedent for FORUM
2. [Bisbee et al. (2023) — Synthetic Replacements?](bisbee-2023.md) — critical companion to Argyle
3. [Fishkin & Luskin (2005)](fishkin-luskin-2005.md) — DP protocol canonical
4. [Park et al. (2024) — Generative Agent Simulations](park-2024.md) — closest neighbor in scale
5. [Hewitt et al. (2024) — Predicting Results of Social Science Experiments](hewitt-2024.md) — calibration of LLM-survey predictions
6. [Bender et al. (2021) — Stochastic Parrots](bender-2021.md) — ethical frame
