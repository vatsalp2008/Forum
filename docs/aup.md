# FORUM Acceptable Use Policy (v0 draft)

This is a v0 draft of the FORUM Acceptable Use Policy. It is in plain language. A reviewed legal version will be required before any commercial deployment.

## What FORUM may be used for

- Academic and policy research into deliberative outcomes
- Public-sector consultation augmentation, with mandatory log publication (see below)
- Civic-tech and methodological development of deliberation tools
- Educational use in classrooms

## What FORUM may not be used for

The following uses are prohibited and the system enforces refusals where technically possible:

1. **Election prediction within 60 days** of a named live ballot measure, candidate election, or referendum.
2. **Persuasive messaging generation** — producing messaging optimized to change the views of any demographic, ideological group, or identified audience.
3. **Voter targeting / ad targeting** — using FORUM outputs to inform voter-contact lists, GOTV decisions, or paid political advertising targeting.
4. **Opposition research** on named individuals or candidates.
5. **Decisions about named individuals** — enforcement actions, employment, credit, benefits eligibility, or any other consequential decision about a specific person.
6. **Single-demographic conversion studies** framed as "how do we convince group X to support policy Y."
7. **Use as a substitute for** real public consultation when the use case calls for democratic legitimacy (FORUM is decision-support, not democratic process).

## Mandatory disclosures for public-sector deployments

Any use of FORUM output in a public-sector decision-support context must:

- Disclose that the deliberation was synthetic and machine-generated
- Publish the full deliberation transcript, persona seed, and methodology version within 90 days
- Cite the calibration backtest results for the measure topic area
- Make a complete methodology document available to journalists and researchers on request

## Refusal of service

FORUM refuses to sell to or run for:

- Parties under active investigation for election interference
- Entities whose stated purpose is partisan voter persuasion
- Foreign-government entities under U.S. sanctions

FORUM does **not** refuse on partisan grounds. Any organization that meets the AUP, regardless of political alignment, is eligible to use the system.

## Reporting violations

If you become aware of a use of FORUM that violates this policy, please open an issue or email the maintainer. The maintainer commits to publishing a yearly transparency report listing flagged misuse and the resolution of each case.
